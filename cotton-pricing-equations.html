Cotton T-Shirts — Multi-Channel Pricing Setup

MNL Choice Model · Profit Equations · M574 Pricing Management

This page lays out two MNL-based profit setups for a cotton t-shirt product line sold across three channels — Retail Chain (1,000 customers), Online Store (700 customers), and Premium Boutique (400 customers). Each setup is shown in standard equation form, followed by a code block you can copy directly into the General Purpose Optimizer on the website.

Unit costs: Basic Cotton Blend = $10, 100% Cotton = $20, Organic Cotton = $30. Reference alternative: No Purchase ($U = 0$).

1. Pooled Analysis (n = 2,100)

A single MNL is estimated on all 2,100 customers, ignoring channel membership. One set of coefficients applies to every customer.

Utilities $$U_{\text{100\%~Cotton}} = 2.2496 - 0.0792 \cdot P_{\text{100\%~Cotton}}$$ $$U_{\text{Basic~Cotton~Blend}} = 2.2462 - 0.1309 \cdot P_{\text{Basic~Cotton~Blend}}$$ $$U_{\text{Organic~Cotton}} = 1.5185 - 0.0429 \cdot P_{\text{Organic~Cotton}}$$
Denominator $$D = 1 + e^{U_{\text{100\%~Cotton}}} + e^{U_{\text{Basic~Cotton~Blend}}} + e^{U_{\text{Organic~Cotton}}}$$
Choice Probabilities $$\Pr(\text{No Purchase}) = \frac{1}{D} \qquad \Pr(j) = \frac{e^{U_j}}{D}$$
Profit $$\pi_{\text{per cust}} = \Pr(\text{100\%}) \cdot (P_{\text{100\%}} - 20) + \Pr(\text{Basic}) \cdot (P_{\text{Basic}} - 10) + \Pr(\text{Org}) \cdot (P_{\text{Org}} - 30)$$ $$\Pi = 2{,}100 \cdot \pi_{\text{per cust}}$$

# Pooled Analysis (n = 2,100)

U_100_Cotton = 2.2496 - 0.0792 * P_100_Cotton
U_Basic_Cotton_Blend = 2.2462 - 0.1309 * P_Basic_Cotton_Blend
U_Organic_Cotton = 1.5185 - 0.0429 * P_Organic_Cotton

Denominator = 1 + exp(U_100_Cotton) + exp(U_Basic_Cotton_Blend) + exp(U_Organic_Cotton)

Pr_No_Purchase = 1 / Denominator
Pr_100_Cotton = exp(U_100_Cotton) / Denominator
Pr_Basic_Cotton_Blend = exp(U_Basic_Cotton_Blend) / Denominator
Pr_Organic_Cotton = exp(U_Organic_Cotton) / Denominator

Profit_Per_Customer = Pr_100_Cotton * (P_100_Cotton - 20) + Pr_Basic_Cotton_Blend * (P_Basic_Cotton_Blend - 10) + Pr_Organic_Cotton * (P_Organic_Cotton - 30)

Profit = Profit_Per_Customer * 2100

2. Channel-by-Channel Analysis

A separate MNL is estimated within each channel, allowing demand sensitivity (and therefore optimal prices) to differ across the three sales venues. Channel profits are summed for the firm-level objective.

Channel 1: Retail Chain n = 1,000

Utilities $$U_{\text{Basic}}^{R} = 3.2219 - 0.1614 \cdot P_{\text{Basic}}^{R}$$ $$U_{\text{100\%}}^{R} = 2.5525 - 0.1085 \cdot P_{\text{100\%}}^{R}$$ $$U_{\text{Org}}^{R} = 2.4886 - 0.0772 \cdot P_{\text{Org}}^{R}$$
Denominator & Probabilities $$D^{R} = 1 + e^{U_{\text{Basic}}^{R}} + e^{U_{\text{100\%}}^{R}} + e^{U_{\text{Org}}^{R}} \qquad \Pr(j)^{R} = \frac{e^{U_j^{R}}}{D^{R}}$$
Channel Profit $$\pi_{\text{per cust}}^{R} = \Pr(\text{Basic})^{R} (P_{\text{Basic}}^{R} - 10) + \Pr(\text{100\%})^{R} (P_{\text{100\%}}^{R} - 20) + \Pr(\text{Org})^{R} (P_{\text{Org}}^{R} - 30)$$ $$\Pi_{R} = 1{,}000 \cdot \pi_{\text{per cust}}^{R}$$

Channel 2: Online Store n = 700

Utilities $$U_{\text{Basic}}^{O} = 1.6488 - 0.1202 \cdot P_{\text{Basic}}^{O}$$ $$U_{\text{100\%}}^{O} = 2.8434 - 0.0767 \cdot P_{\text{100\%}}^{O}$$ $$U_{\text{Org}}^{O} = 1.6097 - 0.0401 \cdot P_{\text{Org}}^{O}$$
Denominator & Probabilities $$D^{O} = 1 + e^{U_{\text{Basic}}^{O}} + e^{U_{\text{100\%}}^{O}} + e^{U_{\text{Org}}^{O}} \qquad \Pr(j)^{O} = \frac{e^{U_j^{O}}}{D^{O}}$$
Channel Profit $$\pi_{\text{per cust}}^{O} = \Pr(\text{Basic})^{O} (P_{\text{Basic}}^{O} - 10) + \Pr(\text{100\%})^{O} (P_{\text{100\%}}^{O} - 20) + \Pr(\text{Org})^{O} (P_{\text{Org}}^{O} - 30)$$ $$\Pi_{O} = 700 \cdot \pi_{\text{per cust}}^{O}$$

Channel 3: Premium Boutique n = 400

Utilities $$U_{\text{Basic}}^{B} = 1.0558 - 0.0871 \cdot P_{\text{Basic}}^{B}$$ $$U_{\text{100\%}}^{B} = 1.5573 - 0.0552 \cdot P_{\text{100\%}}^{B}$$ $$U_{\text{Org}}^{B} = 1.6808 - 0.0281 \cdot P_{\text{Org}}^{B}$$
Denominator & Probabilities $$D^{B} = 1 + e^{U_{\text{Basic}}^{B}} + e^{U_{\text{100\%}}^{B}} + e^{U_{\text{Org}}^{B}} \qquad \Pr(j)^{B} = \frac{e^{U_j^{B}}}{D^{B}}$$
Channel Profit $$\pi_{\text{per cust}}^{B} = \Pr(\text{Basic})^{B} (P_{\text{Basic}}^{B} - 10) + \Pr(\text{100\%})^{B} (P_{\text{100\%}}^{B} - 20) + \Pr(\text{Org})^{B} (P_{\text{Org}}^{B} - 30)$$ $$\Pi_{B} = 400 \cdot \pi_{\text{per cust}}^{B}$$

Total Profit

Aggregated Across Channels $$\Pi = \Pi_{R} + \Pi_{O} + \Pi_{B}$$
