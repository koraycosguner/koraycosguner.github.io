# Unit 3 content audit

This module adds original practice for **Mi Día en la Escuela y Mi Horario** to the existing study site. It does not replace Unit 2 or other subjects.

## Evidence and limits

The supplied build brief defines the vocabulary, grammar scope, class order and 9:00 a.m.–3:45 p.m. school day. No separate Unit 3 teacher worksheet, answer key, exact bell schedule or actual classroom test was supplied with this revision. The activities are original examples in the requested classroom style, not a reproduction of an unseen teacher test. No standards alignment is claimed.

The known class order is preserved: P1 COMPASS; P2 science; P3 mathematics; P4 band; P5 social studies; P8 language arts; P9 Spanish. Individual lesson times are fictional model times. P6 and P7 are explicitly labeled example lunch/break intervals; no unsupplied courses are invented. Staff surnames and the private handoff are omitted from public curriculum data.

The story uses **Tuesday, October 20, 2026**, a fixed example date. Its weekday has been checked with calendar arithmetic. “Today” inside the story refers to that example day, not the viewer’s current date.

## Answer-key and ambiguity decisions

1. **Sobre / encima de:** both can mean on top of. Neither is used as a competing wrong answer against the other. Their English meaning is tested in explicit object-position sentences.
2. **En:** may mean in, at or on depending on context. The reference and translation choice preserve this range instead of claiming it has only one English equivalent.
3. **Adentro / afuera:** the supplied forms are preserved. Contractions such as *adentro del casillero* follow de + el → del.
4. **Empieza / comienza:** both mean starts in these contexts. One is never the distractor for the other.
5. **First day of a month:** *el primero de diciembre* is taught, while the feedback explicitly allows *el uno de diciembre*. The two are not competing choices.
6. **3:45:** *las tres y cuarenta y cinco* is taught. The reference also acknowledges *las cuatro menos cuarto*; the equivalent phrase is not marked wrong.
7. **La clase:** explicitly means classroom when a location is asked for. It also appears as class in schedule contexts. The intended sense is stated or made clear in the sentence.
8. **El recreo / el receso:** described as recess/break time, not a building or room.
9. **La oficina / la oficina del director:** generic office and principal’s office are not used as overlapping alternative meanings for the same question.
10. **Map orientation:** left and right refer to the viewer’s map. Front is toward the map’s bottom. Science is right of the library and between the gym and office; Mateo is in front of the library. The gym is farther back than the library, to its upper right.
11. **Timeline:** the science-finding story happens before second period. The brief’s sample “after lunch” science search was adapted because it conflicts with the supplied P2 science schedule.
12. **Numbers:** counting/page-number forms use uno/veintiuno. The optional reference explains un/una before nouns; students are not penalized for an unintroduced noun-agreement problem.
13. **Fresh time questions:** repeat the skill with changed values (9:15 → 2:15 p.m.; 10:30 → 9:30; 3:45 p.m. → 2:45 p.m.) or a reversed representation for noon/midnight. They do not repeat the identical question and answer under new IDs.
14. **Final independent audit:** SER identity examples and ESTAR location/feeling examples are grouped by the correct verb, not their array position. All tú groups use the same accented spelling. The 1:00 p.m. example says before lunch, consistent with the model lunch beginning at 1:05 p.m.

## Content verification performed

All question templates, story paragraphs, answer labels, explanations, contexts and category coverage were reviewed. Every test-eligible item is self-contained; schedule questions include the relevant order, and the reading question includes its passage. No test relies on remembering a hidden story paragraph.

The 13 dedicated content checks passed. They verify stable/resolvable keys, the full supplied vocabulary inventory, exact conjugations, spelling and accent spot checks, unique synonym-safe alternatives, grammar of assembled passages, consistent weekday/date, all supplied class positions, nonoverlapping sample times, genuine fresh time tasks, test-pool balance and omission of private source details. After the final independent audit fixes, all **34 combined content and learning-engine checks passed** (13 content + 21 engine).

ESLint passed for the new content and content tests. The project TypeScript check passed after integration. Browser interaction, visual layout and production deployment results belong in the final delivery report; this document does not claim those checks were performed by the curriculum audit.
