# Spanish school-day visual direction and prompts

Six original illustrations were created with the built-in imagegen tool. Fictional character
appearances were chosen for this game and are not real-person likenesses. No private references
were used. These selected assets live in `public/quizzes/spanish/school-day/`, optimized as
1280×853 WebP at quality 85. The welcome reuses the friends illustration.

The images create a warm teal, coral and golden school world with a consistent recurring cast.
Instructional facts, backpack counts and the schedule are rendered in accessible application
text and diagrams, not inferred from decorative art. No social-preview image was requested.
