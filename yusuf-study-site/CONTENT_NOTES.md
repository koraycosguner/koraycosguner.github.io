# Spanish content and grading

Verified October 1, 2026 against the supplied Unit 2 vocabulary DOCX (two pages),
subject pronoun/SER chart (two scanned pages), and DOCTOR SER chart (one scanned page).
Originals remain private and are not part of this repository or production output.

- Use the fuller DOCX convention: ellos includes masculine or mixed groups. The PDF
  simplifies that label to boys. Vosotros/vosotras → sois is shown, mainly used in Spain.
- la mesa = table; el escritorio = desk; quince mochilas = fifteen backpacks.
- la goma means glue in this class list; dialects also use it for eraser. Scene context
  makes the intended meaning clear. Both pluma and bolígrafo are valid pen synonyms.
- DOCTOR description/characteristics overlap; no scored question arbitrarily chooses
  between those two labels. Identification, events, material and possession are included.
- A pen can be useful when studying. “Para estudiar necesito un bolígrafo” has no universal
  false answer here and is excluded from scored questions.
- No original backpack picture or official exercise answer key was supplied. Generated
  hay questions and their models use the same inventory displayed to the learner.
- Accent omission in a meaning-focused sentence receives a modeled spelling correction.
  Explicit el/él and tu/tú contrast items require the correct accent. Answer validation
  checks complete accepted responses; finding a correct keyword is insufficient.
- English help translates or contextualizes the question. Answer explanations appear
  after submission. Hints and corrected answers never count as independent first success.
- The preserved earlier big quiz has 60 questions. Practice letter bands: A ≥90%, B ≥80%, C ≥70%, D ≥60%;
  lower scores receive “More practice.” The denominator is the completed session size;
  the numerator is correct, unhinted first responses. No school grade or proficiency
  certification is implied. These earlier quiz results are stored in browser storage.

### Content and scoring

- Ten short teaching modules lead to **30 guided questions**. They cover subject pronouns,
  IN / TO / ABOUT, SER, nouns/articles, HAY, backpack vocabulary, classroom vocabulary,
  subjects/likes, descriptions, and DOCTOR. Pronoun and SER teaching repeatedly connects
  WHO → PRONOUN → SER. Vosotros/vosotras → sois remains a secondary reference form.
- Each mixed round selects **12 questions** in connected school-day order. The selection
  balances personal/group grammar, object functions, inventory-based HAY, noun agreement,
  subjects/likes, reading, descriptions/origin, and a band-room finale. Examples rotate
  without drawing from the separate targeted-review bank.
- The full practice test has **88 questions and 94 scored responses**: six paired questions,
  eight Cierto/Falso items, and 11 questions attached to four short connected passages.
  Every non-Cierto/Falso response has exactly three choices. Assigned backpack/classroom
  vocabulary, all ten subjects, and the assigned adjective set are represented across the
  test, guided practice, and review banks.
- A full-test score counts each step's first answer once, including both responses in a
  paired question. Explanations appear after submission. Test hints and the in-app field
  guide are unavailable while the test is active; there is no timer, life limit, or penalty
  that removes learning access. English context help remains optional.
- Mixed practice and guided practice allow help and retries. First-answer accuracy,
  assistance, and later corrections remain separate; a correction does not rewrite the
  original first response. Guided questions are always supported learning, not independent
  readiness evidence.
- **22 skills** separately track pronouns, individual SER patterns, articles, plural,
  HAY, backpack/classroom vocabulary, subjects, adjectives, origin, reading, SER uses,
  and likes. **Ready** requires two distinct independent examples after the latest miss
  or supported attempt. **A little more practice** and **Review this** identify limited
  evidence and unresolved concerns. These are conservative in-app practice labels, not
  school grades or proficiency certification.
- **40 fresh review questions**, offered in batches of up to six, include at least two
  distinct examples for every tracked skill. Review excludes already displayed examples
  and targets missed or supported concepts. Once fresh review is exhausted, the interface
  offers lessons or more mixed practice instead of claiming a recycled item is new.

The new record is isolated under `yusuf.spanish.proficiency.v1`. It stores local first-answer
evidence, supported attempts, lesson completion, and the active session. Question selection,
submitted feedback, and the two-step cursor survive reload. Resetting this record preserves
the adventure, earlier quiz history, and all other subjects. No learner responses or scores
are committed, published as assets, or sent to a learner-data backend.
