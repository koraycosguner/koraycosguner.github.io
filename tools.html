Analytics and Pricing Tools

Interactive tools for marketing analytics, demand analysis, and pricing strategy.

Created by Dr. Koray Cosguner

Associate Professor of Marketing · John Rau KSB Faculty Fellow

Analytics Tools

Analytics Tool 1

Descriptive Statistics and Confidence Intervals

Upload a CSV to compute descriptive statistics (mean, median, mode, SD, variance, quartiles, range, IQR, and more) for every numeric variable, with a downloadable results table, plus a t-distribution confidence interval for the mean of a variable and confidence level you choose. Runs entirely in your browser via R (WebR).

Koray Cosguner · built with ClaudeLaunch
Analytics Tool 2

Hypothesis Tests: t-tests, ANOVA, Chi-square

Run one-sample, paired, and independent-samples t-tests, one-way ANOVA (F-test), and chi-square test of independence. All powered by base R running in your browser.

Koray Cosguner · built with ClaudeLaunch
Analytics Tool 3

Linear, Poisson, Binary Logistic Regressions

Upload a CSV to preview data, view descriptive statistics, and filter subsets, then fit Linear, Poisson, or Binary Logistic regressions (with variable transformations and interactions) using R in the browser via WebR. View coefficients and the estimated equation, set non-significant coefficients to zero (p < 0.05 or 0.10), make single or batch predictions, and export the R script, estimates, and predictions.

Koray Cosguner · built with ClaudeLaunch
Analytics Tool 4

General Purpose Optimizer

Define a custom system of equations and maximize or minimize any variable over one or more bounded decision variables (with fixed control inputs). Solves in the browser and cross-checks with real R (via webR), then exports the model as R code or an Excel workbook and visualizes the objective.

Koray Cosguner · built with ClaudeLaunch
Analytics Tool 5

Conjoint Fractional Factorial Design

Build conjoint fractional factorial designs in the browser: define attributes and levels, generate a D-optimal subset of profiles via R (AlgDesign) on WebR, and export the profiles and a dummy-coded design matrix as CSV.

Koray Cosguner · built with ClaudeLaunch
Analytics Tool 6

Conjoint Analysis Tool

Analyzes ratings-based conjoint survey data: estimates per-respondent part-worth utilities via OLS regression, computes attribute importance, segments respondents by their key attributes, and runs a choice simulator that reports individual choices, choice probabilities, and aggregate market shares (first-choice and share of preference).

Koray Cosguner · built with ClaudeLaunch
Analytics Tool 7

Latent Class Regression

Estimates segment-level regression models via finite mixture modeling (the flexmix package in R), supporting Linear, Poisson, and Binary Logistic families. Reports per-segment coefficients (with standard errors and p-values), segment sizes, fit statistics (AIC, BIC, log-likelihood), consumer/household segment assignments, and an exportable R script.

Koray Cosguner · built with ClaudeLaunch
Analytics Tool 8

Multinomial and Conditional Logistic Regressions

Estimate multinomial and conditional logit choice models with the mlogit R package, running entirely in your browser (no R install, data stays local). Upload long or wide CSV data, specify alternative-specific and choice-invariant variables, then view coefficients, fit statistics, prediction equations, and interactive choice probabilities, with CSV and R-script export.

Koray Cosguner · built with ClaudeLaunch

Pricing Tools

Pricing Tool 1

Pricing Effectiveness Matrix

A weighted self-assessment that scores an organization's pricing capability across strategy, execution, and analytics, using adjustable factor weights and 1-to-5 ratings to produce a normalized score and maturity band.

Adapted from Rockney Walters (Professor of Marketing), Stephen Brown (MBA), and Will Charron (MBA).
Koray Cosguner · built with Gemini 3.0Launch
Pricing Tool 2

Qualitative Elasticity Assessment

Estimates a product's price elasticity by weighting and rating the 10 price-sensitivity drivers from Nagle and Muller's "The Strategy and Tactics of Pricing," returning a weighted score classified as elastic, unitary, or inelastic.

Koray Cosguner · built with Gemini 3.0Launch
Pricing Tool 3

Price Elasticity Calculator

Computes price elasticity of demand, revenue, and profit from two price/quantity points and a variable cost, then reports before/after revenue, margin, and profit with strategic price/volume and margin/volume insights.

Koray Cosguner · built with Gemini 3.0Launch
Pricing Tool 4

Income Elasticity (YED)

Calculates Income Elasticity of Demand from before/after income and quantity values, then classifies the product as a Necessity, Luxury, or Inferior good.

Koray Cosguner · built with Gemini 3.0Launch
Pricing Tool 5

Model-Based Price Elasticity Calculator

Enter an estimated demand equation (with fixed control variables), two price points, and unit cost to compute price elasticities of demand, revenue, and profit, plus a before/after table of quantity, revenue, margin, and profit.

Koray Cosguner · built with ClaudeLaunch
Pricing Tool 6

Price Optimizer (Linear)

Solver for linear demand (Q = A + BP): computes the optimal price under revenue maximization vs. profit maximization, with side-by-side metrics and revenue/profit curves.

Koray Cosguner · built with Gemini 3.0Launch
Pricing Tool 7

Single-Point Price Optimizer

Finds the profit-maximizing price from a single price/quantity point, an estimated elasticity, and variable cost using a constant-elasticity demand model. Outputs current vs. optimal price, profit, and a profit-vs-price chart.

Koray Cosguner · built with Gemini 3.0Launch
Pricing Tool 8

Channel Pricing Optimizer

Optimizes producer pricing in a vertical channel under three retailer types (vertically integrated, cost-plus markup, and strategic/Stackelberg), using a custom linear or non-linear demand function. Reports optimal wholesale and retail prices, profits, and the double-marginalization efficiency loss, with a comparison table, profit chart, and copyable R code.

Koray Cosguner · built with ClaudeLaunch
Pricing Tool 9

Pricing with Substitute or Complementary Products

Solves optimal pricing for two substitute or complementary products across four channel scenarios (single owner vs. competing brands, selling direct vs. through a common retailer), comparing prices, quantities, and profits. Demand parameters can be entered manually or estimated from uploaded data via in-browser OLS regression, with built-in demand-prediction and elasticity calculators.

Koray Cosguner · built with ClaudeLaunch
Pricing Tool 10

Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter

Discover optimal pricing and acceptable price ranges using customer survey data. Upload responses to four price sensitivity questions and get key price points instantly.

Koray Cosguner · built with ClaudeLaunch
Pricing Tool 11

Gabor-Granger Price Analysis

Find the revenue-maximizing (and, with unit cost, profit-maximizing) price from individual willingness-to-pay data. Enter WTP values manually or upload a CSV to build a demand curve, with charts, a results table, and CSV/PNG export.

Koray Cosguner · built with ClaudeLaunch
Pricing Tool 12

Pricing Survey Builder

Instructor console to build, share, and manage Van Westendorp (PSM) and Gabor-Granger pricing surveys: collects student responses in real time and exports them (or loads them directly) into the matching analysis tools.

Instructor only. Uses Firebase for response storage.
Koray Cosguner · built with ClaudeLaunch
