For each respondent, sum the intercept plus the part-worths of the selected levels. Excel formula: =SUM(C37:C45) (summing the intercept + each attribute’s selected level).

Question 58 pts

Choice Probability (Share of Utility)

P(Budget) = U(Budget) / (U(Budget) + U(Performance)). Excel: =C46/(C46+C58)

Question 68 pts
Predict the market shares by aggregating respondent-level choices. What would be the market share of the Budget car?

Choice Aggregation

Count the respondents who choose Budget: Akasuki, Xi, Yosef, Abayomi = 4 out of 8.

Market Share (Budget) = 4 / 8 = 0.50

Excel: =SUM(C61:J61)/8 where row 61 contains 1 if Budget, 0 if Performance.

✓ Answer: 0.50
Question 78 pts
Predict the market shares by aggregating respondent-level choice probabilities. What would be the market share of the Budget car?

Choice Probability Aggregation

Average the P(Budget) values across all 8 respondents:

(0.03 + 0.42 + 0.61 + 0.61 + 0.57 + 0.77 + 0.49 + 0.49) / 8 = 3.99 / 8 = 0.50

Excel: =AVERAGE(C62:J62)

Both aggregation methods (Q6: choice-based, Q7: probability-based) yield approximately the same market share of 0.50, indicating an evenly split market between the Budget and Performance cars.

✓ Answer: 0.50
