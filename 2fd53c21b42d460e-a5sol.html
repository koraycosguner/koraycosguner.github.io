Q4 — Utility of Laptop A

U(A) = 7.376 + 11.086 + 8.795 + 31.506 + 0 + 17.776 + (−4.953) = 71.59

Q5 — Choice

U(A) = 71.59 > U(B) = 32.75, so Aamir chooses Laptop A.

Q6 — Choice Probability (Share of Utility)

P(A) = U(A) / (U(A) + U(B)) = 71.59 / (71.59 + 32.75) = 71.59 / 104.34 = 0.69

✓ Q4: 71.59    Q5: Laptop A    Q6: 0.69
