Product Portfolio Pricing & Channel Level Pricing

Good–Better–Best Portfolio · Performance Driven Pricing

Tailor & Co. sells dress shirts across three distinct channels with a tiered product portfolio. Because customer bases differ across venues, preferences and price sensitivities can vary — so we compare two estimation strategies on the same 2,100−customer dataset: (1) a pooled product choice model and (2) channel-level product choice models estimated separately. Each setup is shown in standard equation form, with code blocks ready to paste into Tool 8: General Purpose Optimizer.

Pooled Product Choice Model

n = 2,100

A single product choice model is estimated on all 2,100 customers, ignoring channel membership. One set of coefficients applies to every customer regardless of where they shop.

Utilities

Each alternative's deterministic utility is a linear function of its own price. The intercept captures the alternative's intrinsic appeal; the slope is the price-sensitivity coefficient (more negative = more sensitive to price).

$$U_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}} = 2.2462 - 0.1309 \cdot P_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}}$$ $$U_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}} = 2.2496 - 0.0792 \cdot P_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}}$$ $$U_{\text{Organic Cotton}} = 1.5185 - 0.0429 \cdot P_{\text{Organic Cotton}}$$
Denominator

The denominator normalizes the choice shares so they sum to one. The leading 1 corresponds to exp(0), the utility of the No Purchase outside option (the reference level).

$$D = 1 + \exp(U_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}}) + \exp(U_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}}) + \exp(U_{\text{Organic Cotton}})$$
Choice Probabilities

In the product choice model, the choice probability of each alternative is its exp(utility) divided by the denominator. The four probabilities are mutually exclusive and sum to one.

$$\Pr(\text{No Purchase}) = \frac{1}{D}$$ $$\Pr(\text{Basic Cotton Blend}) = \frac{\exp(U_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}})}{D}$$ $$\Pr(100\%\,\text{Cotton}) = \frac{\exp(U_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}})}{D}$$ $$\Pr(\text{Organic Cotton}) = \frac{\exp(U_{\text{Organic Cotton}})}{D}$$
Profit

Per-customer profit is the probability-weighted sum of unit margins (price minus unit cost) across the three products. Total firm profit scales by market size (2,100 customers).

$$\begin{aligned} \text{Profit}_{\text{per Customer}} = \;& \Pr(\text{Basic Cotton Blend}) \cdot (P_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}} - 10) \\ + \;& \Pr(100\%\,\text{Cotton}) \cdot (P_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}} - 20) \\ + \;& \Pr(\text{Organic Cotton}) \cdot (P_{\text{Organic Cotton}} - 30) \end{aligned}$$ $$\text{Profit} = 2{,}100 \cdot \text{Profit}_{\text{per Customer}}$$

# Pooled Analysis (n = 2,100)

U_Basic_Cotton_Blend = 2.2462 - 0.1309 * P_Basic_Cotton_Blend
U_100_Cotton = 2.2496 - 0.0792 * P_100_Cotton
U_Organic_Cotton = 1.5185 - 0.0429 * P_Organic_Cotton

Denominator = 1 + exp(U_Basic_Cotton_Blend) + exp(U_100_Cotton) + exp(U_Organic_Cotton)

Pr_No_Purchase = 1 / Denominator
Pr_Basic_Cotton_Blend = exp(U_Basic_Cotton_Blend) / Denominator
Pr_100_Cotton = exp(U_100_Cotton) / Denominator
Pr_Organic_Cotton = exp(U_Organic_Cotton) / Denominator

Profit_Per_Customer = Pr_Basic_Cotton_Blend * (P_Basic_Cotton_Blend - 10) + Pr_100_Cotton * (P_100_Cotton - 20) + Pr_Organic_Cotton * (P_Organic_Cotton - 30)

Profit = Profit_Per_Customer * 2100

Channel-Level Product Choice Models

3 channels

A separate product choice model is estimated within each channel, allowing intercepts (intrinsic appeal) and slopes (price sensitivity) to differ across the three sales venues. Channel profits are then summed for the firm-level objective.

Channel 1

Retail Chain

1,000 customers
Utilities — Retail Chain

Estimated only on the 1,000 Retail Chain customers. Variables are channel-specific (each price decision applies only inside this channel).

$$U^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}} = 3.2219 - 0.1614 \cdot P^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}}$$ $$U^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}} = 2.5525 - 0.1085 \cdot P^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}}$$ $$U^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{\text{Organic Cotton}} = 2.4886 - 0.0772 \cdot P^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{\text{Organic Cotton}}$$
Denominator — Retail Chain

Normalizing constant for the Retail Chain choice probabilities; the leading 1 is the No Purchase outside option.

$$\begin{aligned} D^{\text{Retail Chain}} = \;& 1 + \exp(U^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}}) \\ + \;& \exp(U^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}}) + \exp(U^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{\text{Organic Cotton}}) \end{aligned}$$
Choice Probabilities — Retail Chain

Choice probabilities computed within the Retail Chain channel.

$$\Pr(\text{No Purchase})^{\text{Retail Chain}} = \frac{1}{D^{\text{Retail Chain}}}$$ $$\Pr(\text{Basic Cotton Blend})^{\text{Retail Chain}} = \frac{\exp(U^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}})}{D^{\text{Retail Chain}}}$$ $$\Pr(100\%\,\text{Cotton})^{\text{Retail Chain}} = \frac{\exp(U^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}})}{D^{\text{Retail Chain}}}$$ $$\Pr(\text{Organic Cotton})^{\text{Retail Chain}} = \frac{\exp(U^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{\text{Organic Cotton}})}{D^{\text{Retail Chain}}}$$
Channel Profit — Retail Chain

Per-customer profit is the probability-weighted margin within this channel. Channel-level profit scales by 1,000 customers.

$$\begin{aligned} \text{Profit}^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{\text{per Customer}} = \;& \Pr(\text{Basic Cotton Blend})^{\text{Retail Chain}} \cdot (P^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}} - 10) \\ + \;& \Pr(100\%\,\text{Cotton})^{\text{Retail Chain}} \cdot (P^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}} - 20) \\ + \;& \Pr(\text{Organic Cotton})^{\text{Retail Chain}} \cdot (P^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{\text{Organic Cotton}} - 30) \end{aligned}$$ $$\text{Profit}^{\text{Retail Chain}} = 1{,}000 \cdot \text{Profit}^{\text{Retail Chain}}_{\text{per Customer}}$$
Channel 2

Online Store

700 customers
Utilities — Online Store

Estimated only on the 700 Online Store customers. Coefficients differ from the Retail Chain because online shoppers tend to have different preferences and price sensitivities.

$$U^{\text{Online Store}}_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}} = 1.6488 - 0.1202 \cdot P^{\text{Online Store}}_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}}$$ $$U^{\text{Online Store}}_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}} = 2.8434 - 0.0767 \cdot P^{\text{Online Store}}_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}}$$ $$U^{\text{Online Store}}_{\text{Organic Cotton}} = 1.6097 - 0.0401 \cdot P^{\text{Online Store}}_{\text{Organic Cotton}}$$
Denominator — Online Store

Normalizing constant for the Online Store choice probabilities.

$$\begin{aligned} D^{\text{Online Store}} = \;& 1 + \exp(U^{\text{Online Store}}_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}}) \\ + \;& \exp(U^{\text{Online Store}}_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}}) + \exp(U^{\text{Online Store}}_{\text{Organic Cotton}}) \end{aligned}$$
Choice Probabilities — Online Store

Choice probabilities computed within the Online Store channel.

$$\Pr(\text{No Purchase})^{\text{Online Store}} = \frac{1}{D^{\text{Online Store}}}$$ $$\Pr(\text{Basic Cotton Blend})^{\text{Online Store}} = \frac{\exp(U^{\text{Online Store}}_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}})}{D^{\text{Online Store}}}$$ $$\Pr(100\%\,\text{Cotton})^{\text{Online Store}} = \frac{\exp(U^{\text{Online Store}}_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}})}{D^{\text{Online Store}}}$$ $$\Pr(\text{Organic Cotton})^{\text{Online Store}} = \frac{\exp(U^{\text{Online Store}}_{\text{Organic Cotton}})}{D^{\text{Online Store}}}$$
Channel Profit — Online Store

Channel-level profit scales by 700 customers.

$$\begin{aligned} \text{Profit}^{\text{Online Store}}_{\text{per Customer}} = \;& \Pr(\text{Basic Cotton Blend})^{\text{Online Store}} \cdot (P^{\text{Online Store}}_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}} - 10) \\ + \;& \Pr(100\%\,\text{Cotton})^{\text{Online Store}} \cdot (P^{\text{Online Store}}_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}} - 20) \\ + \;& \Pr(\text{Organic Cotton})^{\text{Online Store}} \cdot (P^{\text{Online Store}}_{\text{Organic Cotton}} - 30) \end{aligned}$$ $$\text{Profit}^{\text{Online Store}} = 700 \cdot \text{Profit}^{\text{Online Store}}_{\text{per Customer}}$$
Channel 3

Premium Boutique

400 customers
Utilities — Premium Boutique

Estimated only on the 400 Premium Boutique customers. Lower price-sensitivity slopes typically reflect a less price-elastic, premium clientele.

$$U^{\text{Premium Boutique}}_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}} = 1.0558 - 0.0871 \cdot P^{\text{Premium Boutique}}_{\text{Basic Cotton Blend}}$$ $$U^{\text{Premium Boutique}}_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}} = 1.5573 - 0.0552 \cdot P^{\text{Premium Boutique}}_{100\%\,\text{Cotton}}$$ $$U^{\text{Premium Boutique}}_{\text{Organic Cotton}} = 1.6808 - 0.0281 \cdot P^{\text{Premium Boutique}}_{\text{Organic Cotton}}$$

Total Profit Across Channels

Firm-Level Objective

Total firm profit is the sum of the three channel profits. Because each channel's prices appear only in its own profit term, the channels are independent — they can be optimized separately and the optima summed.

$$\text{Profit} = \text{Profit}^{\text{Retail Chain}} + \text{Profit}^{\text{Online Store}} + \text{Profit}^{\text{Premium Boutique}}$$

# Setting up the Profit from the Retail Chain (n = 1,000)

U_Basic_Cotton_Blend_Retail_Chain = 3.2219 - 0.1614 * P_Basic_Cotton_Blend_Retail_Chain
U_100_Cotton_Retail_Chain = 2.5525 - 0.1085 * P_100_Cotton_Retail_Chain
U_Organic_Cotton_Retail_Chain = 2.4886 - 0.0772 * P_Organic_Cotton_Retail_Chain

Denominator_Retail_Chain = 1 + exp(U_Basic_Cotton_Blend_Retail_Chain) + exp(U_100_Cotton_Retail_Chain) + exp(U_Organic_Cotton_Retail_Chain)

Pr_Basic_Cotton_Blend_Retail_Chain = exp(U_Basic_Cotton_Blend_Retail_Chain) / Denominator_Retail_Chain
Pr_100_Cotton_Retail_Chain = exp(U_100_Cotton_Retail_Chain) / Denominator_Retail_Chain
Pr_Organic_Cotton_Retail_Chain = exp(U_Organic_Cotton_Retail_Chain) / Denominator_Retail_Chain

Profit_Per_Customer_Retail_Chain = Pr_Basic_Cotton_Blend_Retail_Chain * (P_Basic_Cotton_Blend_Retail_Chain - 10) + Pr_100_Cotton_Retail_Chain * (P_100_Cotton_Retail_Chain - 20) + Pr_Organic_Cotton_Retail_Chain * (P_Organic_Cotton_Retail_Chain - 30)

Profit_Retail_Chain = Profit_Per_Customer_Retail_Chain * 1000

# Setting up the Profit from the Online Store (n = 700)

U_Basic_Cotton_Blend_Online_Store = 1.6488 - 0.1202 * P_Basic_Cotton_Blend_Online_Store
U_100_Cotton_Online_Store = 2.8434 - 0.0767 * P_100_Cotton_Online_Store
U_Organic_Cotton_Online_Store = 1.6097 - 0.0401 * P_Organic_Cotton_Online_Store

Denominator_Online_Store = 1 + exp(U_Basic_Cotton_Blend_Online_Store) + exp(U_100_Cotton_Online_Store) + exp(U_Organic_Cotton_Online_Store)

Pr_Basic_Cotton_Blend_Online_Store = exp(U_Basic_Cotton_Blend_Online_Store) / Denominator_Online_Store
Pr_100_Cotton_Online_Store = exp(U_100_Cotton_Online_Store) / Denominator_Online_Store
Pr_Organic_Cotton_Online_Store = exp(U_Organic_Cotton_Online_Store) / Denominator_Online_Store

Profit_Per_Customer_Online_Store = Pr_Basic_Cotton_Blend_Online_Store * (P_Basic_Cotton_Blend_Online_Store - 10) + Pr_100_Cotton_Online_Store * (P_100_Cotton_Online_Store - 20) + Pr_Organic_Cotton_Online_Store * (P_Organic_Cotton_Online_Store - 30)

Profit_Online_Store = Profit_Per_Customer_Online_Store * 700

# Setting up the Profit from the Premium Boutique (n = 400)
