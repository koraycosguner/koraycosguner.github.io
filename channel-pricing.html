1 Model Setup

Channel Structure
Producer Profit = (W − C) · Q
Retailer Profit = (P − W) · Q
Q = f(P)   (demand as function of retail price)

Notation

P = Retail price (to consumers)
W = Wholesale price (producer to retailer)
C = Producer's marginal cost
Q = Quantity demanded

Solution Method

For linear demand (Q = a − b·P), exact analytical solutions are provided using first-order conditions. For non-linear demand functions, solutions are approximate, obtained via grid search optimization.

Enter demand as a function of P. Use: +, -, *, /, ^, exp(), log(), sqrt()

Vertically Integrated

📊

Cost-Plus Retailer

Strategic Retailer

Retailer optimizes own profit given W. Producer anticipates this response.
