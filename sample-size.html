Confidence Interval for a Population Mean

The interval is the sample mean plus and minus the sampling error, z · s / √n. Use σ if you know it, otherwise the sample s.

Sample Size for a Target Sampling Error

Fix how much sampling error you are willing to live with, then invert the sampling-error formula for n. Always round up: a fractional respondent is not a respondent.

Ad Hoc Methods

Used when experience or the budget, rather than a precision target, sets the sample size.

Minimum per group

Each major group should contain at least this many respondents once the sample is split proportionally.

Minimum per subgroup

When subgroups will also be compared, each one needs 20 to 50 respondents. The smallest subgroup drives the total.

Budget constraint

Whatever is left after fixed costs, divided by the cost of one interview. Round down: you cannot afford a partial respondent.
