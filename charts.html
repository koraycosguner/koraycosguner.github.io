Charts and Frequency Distributions

  • Upload a CSV, pick a figure, and shape it with live controls, no coding required
  • Fourteen chart types: bar, grouped and stacked bar, lollipop, pie, donut, histogram, density, box, violin, line, area, scatter and heatmap
  • Themes, color palettes, labels, legends, sorting and data labels, all updating as you click
  • Export as PNG, JPEG or SVG, and download the frequency table behind any count-based figure
