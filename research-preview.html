Management Science · 2022 · Equal Authorship

Dynamic Pricing for New Products Using a Utility-Based Generalization of the Bass Diffusion Model

Koray Cosguner and P. B. Seetharaman

The Bass Model has an excellent track record in new-product sales forecasting, but its use for optimal dynamic pricing is limited because the Generalized Bass Model uses only percentage changes in marketing variables. We employ a utility-based extension that yields normative prescriptions for both the introductory price and the post-launch price path, outperforming the GBM across four product categories and extending naturally to competitive, multi-product cases.
