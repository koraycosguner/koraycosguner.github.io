The Kiseki

Hand-Polished Japanese Whiskey Glasses

Elevate the Ritual.

Whiskey isn’t just a drink; it’s a pause button on a busy world. The Kiseki Collection was designed to honor that moment. Inspired by the sharp, geometric precision of traditional Edo Kiriko craftsmanship, these glasses transform a simple pour into a sensory experience.

The deep, diamond-cut lattice pattern captures the ambient light of your home bar, making your golden spirits glow from within. With a substantial, weighted base, they offer a reassuring stability in the hand, perfect for the slow sip of a single malt or the crisp finish of a classic Highball.

Why you’ll love them

  • Weighted Balance: A heavy bottom (approx. 350g) provides a premium, grounded feel.
  • Brilliant Clarity: Crafted from ultra-clear, lead-free crystal glass to showcase the true color of your spirits.
  • The Perfect Pour: The wide brim accommodates large ice spheres or whiskey stones without crowding the glass.
